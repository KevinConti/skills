I finally have an intuition on the future of IDEs in an AI world. It won't be a code browser, and it certainly won't be a TUI. We are a derivation behind where we need to be. Our interactions are still 1:1 human-to-agent. Some crazies are multiplexing 1:30 but there is clearly a low limit. The next IDE will solve the problem by breaking free of this relationship entirely. Instead of co-working with the agent, we will be co-developing the **asynchronous system** of agentic workflows that compose together to iteratively verify and harden a codebase. Once we get here, we will look back and laugh at how we used to open an entire terminal just to talk to a single agent and thought we were productive.
